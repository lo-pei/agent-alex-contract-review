**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H63
**Date**: 18 July 2026
**Place**: Ningbo, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Ningbo Cixing Knitting Machine Co., Ltd., Ningbo, China
**Buyer**: Tejido Sur S.A., Lima, Peru

### 第2条 Goods

Computerised flat knitting machines, model CX-72, quantity 40 units, configuration per Annex B.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 520,000.
3.2 Trade term: **FCA Ningbo, Incoterms 2020**.
3.3 Delivery shall be completed by handing the Goods to the carrier nominated by the Buyer at
the Seller's premises in Ningbo.

### 第4条 Delivery Time

4.1 The Seller shall complete the delivery under Clause 3.3 on or before 30 September 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer from the moment the Goods are handed over to the
carrier under Clause 3.3.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 10 days after arrival at Lima.
6.2 Any defect shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay an advance payment of 20% of the contract price by T/T within ten days
after signing, and the remaining 80% against the shipping documents.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 A party affected by force majeure shall give written notice within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration at the China International Economic and
Trade Arbitration Commission, Shanghai.
9.3 The award shall be final and binding upon both parties.
