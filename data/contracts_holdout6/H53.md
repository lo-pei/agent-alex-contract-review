**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H53
**Date**: 3 June 2026
**Place**: Qingdao, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Qingdao Haimo Rubber Products Co., Ltd., Qingdao, China
**Buyer**: Nordkap Autoteile GmbH, Bremen, Germany

### 第2条 Goods

Steel-belted radial tyres, size 295/80 R22.5, quantity 1,200 pieces, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 214,800.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Qingdao, Incoterms 2020**.
3.3 The Seller shall arrange and pay for the ocean freight from Qingdao to Bremen, and the
freight cost is included in the price stated in Clause 3.1.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer at Qingdao
Port not later than 25 July 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be taken out by the Buyer at its own expense.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 21 days after arrival at Bremen.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration at the China International Economic and
Trade Arbitration Commission, Qingdao.
9.3 The arbitral award shall be final and binding on both parties.
