**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H57
**Date**: 24 June 2026
**Place**: Guangzhou, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Guangzhou Pearl River Ceramics Co., Ltd., Guangzhou, China
**Buyer**: Lagos Building Materials Ltd., Lagos, Nigeria

### 第2条 Goods

Porcelain floor tiles, size 600 x 600 mm, quantity 24,000 cartons, shade and packing per
Annex 2.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 132,000.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Apapa, Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods at Nansha Port on or before 5 September 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods for 110% of the contract price under ICC(C) cover.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 21 days after arrival at Apapa.
6.2 Any claim shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 A party affected by force majeure shall give written notice within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Dispute Resolution

9.1 Any dispute arising out of or in relation to this Contract shall be finally settled by
arbitration in Hong Kong under the HKIAC Administered Arbitration Rules, and the seat of the
arbitration shall be Hong Kong.
9.2 The arbitral award shall be final and binding upon the parties.
