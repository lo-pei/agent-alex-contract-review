**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H59
**Date**: 2 July 2026
**Place**: Xiamen, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Xiamen Seaview Garment Co., Ltd., Xiamen, China
**Buyer**: Antwerp Fashion Distribution NV, Antwerp, Belgium

### 第2条 Goods

Cotton knitted sweaters, style SV-318, quantity 36,000 pieces, colour assortment per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 98,300.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Antwerp**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods at Xiamen Port on or before 20 September 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods for 110% of the contract price under ICC(C) cover and
shall assign the policy to the Buyer.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 21 days after arrival at Antwerp.
6.2 Any claim shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 A party affected by force majeure shall give written notice within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration at the China International Economic and
Trade Arbitration Commission, Xiamen.
9.3 The award shall be final and binding upon both parties.
