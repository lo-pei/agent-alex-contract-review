**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H62
**Date**: 14 July 2026
**Place**: Hangzhou, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Hangzhou Precision Gears Co., Ltd., Hangzhou, China
**Buyer**: Hansa Antriebstechnik GmbH, Hamburg, Germany

### 第2条 Goods

Precision spur gears, model HG-45, quantity 12,000 pieces, hardness and tolerance per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: EUR 205,000.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Hamburg, Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods at Ningbo Port on or before 25 August 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods for 110% of the contract price under ICC(C) cover and
shall deliver the policy to the Buyer together with the shipping documents.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Hamburg.
6.2 Written notice of any defect shall be given within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
remaining 70% by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 A party affected by force majeure shall notify the other party in writing within 15 days
after the event, and performance shall be suspended for the duration of the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration in Hamburg under the Rules of the
German Institution of Arbitration (DIS).
9.3 The award shall be final and binding upon both parties.
