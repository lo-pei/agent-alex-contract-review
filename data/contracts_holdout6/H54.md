**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H54
**Date**: 9 June 2026
**Place**: Shanghai, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Shanghai Sunward Industrial Belting Co., Ltd., Shanghai, China
**Buyer**: Rotterdam Machine Parts B.V., Rotterdam, the Netherlands

### 第2条 Goods

Conveyor belts, model SW-880, quantity 640 rolls, width 1,200 mm, specification per Annex 1.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: EUR 188,400.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Rotterdam, Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods at Shanghai Port on or before 30 July 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall effect marine insurance in favour of the Buyer under Institute Cargo
Clauses (A), and shall hand the policy over to the Buyer together with the shipping documents.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Rotterdam.
6.2 Any non-conformity shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
remaining 70% by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 A party affected by force majeure shall notify the other party in writing within 15 days.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration at the China International Economic and
Trade Arbitration Commission, Shanghai.
9.3 The award shall be final and binding upon both parties.
