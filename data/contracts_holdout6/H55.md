**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H55
**Date**: 15 June 2026
**Place**: Shenzhen, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Shenzhen Brightway Electronics Co., Ltd., Shenzhen, China
**Buyer**: Meridian Digital Pte. Ltd., Singapore

### 第2条 Goods

USB-C docking stations, model BW-710, quantity 9,000 units, packing per Annex B.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 243,000.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Singapore, Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods from Yantian Port in two equal lots, the first lot on or
before 5 August 2026 and the second lot on or before 20 August 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods for 110% of the contract price under ICC(C) cover and
shall deliver the policy to the Buyer.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Singapore.
6.2 Written notice of any defect shall be given within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 A party affected by force majeure shall give written notice to the other party within
15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract shall be governed by the laws of Singapore.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Contract shall be settled by
arbitration in Singapore under the SIAC Rules or, at the Buyer's option, by proceedings in the
courts of Singapore.
9.3 The award shall be final and binding on both parties.
