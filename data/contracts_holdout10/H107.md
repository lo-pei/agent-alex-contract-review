**PURCHASE ORDER**

**PO No.**: H107
**Date**: 10 November 2026

**Buyer**: Nordic Components AB, Gothenburg, Sweden
**Seller**: Qingdao Ruixin Hardware Co., Ltd., Qingdao, China

### 1. Goods

Galvanised steel pipe fittings, model RX-45, quantity 12,000 pieces, packed in export cartons.

### 2. Price and Trade Term

2.1 Total order value: USD 72,000.
2.2 Trade term: **FOB Qingdao, Incoterms® 2020**.
2.3 The Goods shall be shipped as one FCL container (40-foot HQ) and delivered to the container
yard at Qingdao Port.

### 3. Delivery

3.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer not later than
15 December 2026.

### 4. Insurance

4.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer at its own cost.

### 5. Inspection and Notice

5.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Gothenburg.
5.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 6. Payment

6.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing;
the balance of 70% shall be paid against a copy of the bill of lading.

### 7. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

7.1 This Order is governed by the laws of Singapore.
7.2 Any dispute arising out of this Order shall be finally settled by arbitration administered
by SIAC under the SIAC Rules, and the award shall be final and binding.
