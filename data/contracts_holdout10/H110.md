**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H110
**Date**: 13 November 2026
**Place**: Ningbo, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Ningbo Zhonghe Textile Machinery Co., Ltd., Ningbo, China
**Buyer**: Textile Engineering (Pvt) Ltd., Colombo, Sri Lanka

### 第2条 Goods

Computerised flat knitting machines, model ZH-252, quantity 6 units, packed in wooden cases.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 168,000.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Ningbo, Incoterms® 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer at Ningbo Port
not later than 18 December 2026.
4.2 The Buyer shall give the Seller at least 14 days' notice of the vessel name and laycan.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer at its own cost.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Colombo.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 20% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing;
the balance of 80% shall be paid against a copy of the bill of lading.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided
that written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Dispute Resolution

9.1 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration
administered by SIAC under the SIAC Rules, and the award shall be final and binding.
9.2 The seat of arbitration shall be Singapore and the language of the arbitration shall be English.
