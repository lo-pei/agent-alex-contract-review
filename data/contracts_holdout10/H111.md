**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H111
**Date**: 14 November 2026
**Place**: Zhuhai, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Zhuhai Sunny Electric Appliances Co., Ltd., Zhuhai, China
**Buyer**: Rotterdam Home Appliance BV, Rotterdam, Netherlands

### 第2条 Goods

Stainless steel electric kettles, model SE-1800, quantity 8,000 pieces, packed in export cartons.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 112,000.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Rotterdam, Incoterms® 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods from Zhuhai Port not later than 22 December 2026.
4.2 The Buyer shall give the Seller at least 14 days' notice of the vessel name and laycan.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods for 110% of the invoice value against Institute Cargo
Clauses (A), and the insurance policy shall be transferred to the Buyer with the bill of lading.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after discharge at Rotterdam.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing;
the balance of 70% shall be paid against a copy of the bill of lading.

### 第8条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

8.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of Singapore.
8.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration administered
by SIAC under the SIAC Rules, and the award shall be final and binding.
