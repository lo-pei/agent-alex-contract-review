**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H101
**Date**: 3 November 2026
**Place**: Xiamen, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Xiamen Lianhai Ceramics Co., Ltd., Xiamen, China
**Buyer**: Red Sea Building Materials LLC, Djibouti

### 第2条 Goods

Glazed porcelain floor tiles, model LH-880, 600 x 600 mm, quantity 18,000 square metres,
packed in wooden crates on pallets.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 96,000.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Xiamen, Incoterms® 2020**.
3.3 Notwithstanding the FOB term, all costs of carriage from Xiamen to Djibouti, including
ocean freight, shall be defrayed by the Seller.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer at Xiamen
Port not later than 20 December 2026.
4.2 The Buyer shall give the Seller at least 14 days' notice of the vessel name and laycan.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer at its own cost.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Djibouti.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 20% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing;
the balance of 80% shall be paid against a copy of the bill of lading.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided
that written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of Singapore.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration
administered by SIAC under the SIAC Rules. The award shall be final and binding.
