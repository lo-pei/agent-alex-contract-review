**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H82
**Date**: 17 October 2026
**Place**: Qingdao, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Qingdao Harbour Rubber Products Co., Ltd., Qingdao, China
**Buyer**: Pacific Auto Parts Inc., Long Beach, United States

### 第2条 Goods

Rubber mud flaps for light trucks, model QD-77, quantity 20,000 pairs, packed in cartons
according to Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 57,900.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Busan, Incoterms® 2020**.
3.3 The Goods shall be stuffed into one 40-foot FCL container at the Busan terminal nominated
by the Buyer.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer at Busan Port
not later than 5 December 2026.
4.2 The risk of loss of or damage to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Goods are on board
the vessel.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer at its own expense.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Long Beach.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration in New York
under the AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules, and the award shall be final and binding.
