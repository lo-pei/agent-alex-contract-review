**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H87
**Date**: 25 October 2026
**Place**: Ningbo, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Ningbo Greenfield Bamboo Ware Co., Ltd., Ningbo, China
**Buyer**: Delta Kitchen Supply B.V., Rotterdam, the Netherlands

### 第2条 Goods

Bamboo kitchen utensils, model GF-118, 8 pieces per set, quantity 15,000 sets, packed in kraft
boxes according to Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 66,400.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Rotterdam, Incoterms® 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods from Ningbo Port not later than 15 December 2026.
4.2 The risk of loss of or damage to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Goods are on board
the vessel at the port of shipment.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods for 110% of the invoice value against Institute Cargo
Clauses (C), and the policy shall be assignable to the Buyer.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Rotterdam.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance of 70% by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the Netherlands.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration in Amsterdam
under the NAI Arbitration Rules, and the award shall be final and binding.
