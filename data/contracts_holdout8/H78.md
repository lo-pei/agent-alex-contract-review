**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H78
**Date**: 21 October 2026
**Place**: Ningbo, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Ningbo Brightway Lighting Co., Ltd., Ningbo, China
**Buyer**: Lagos Power Solutions Ltd., Lagos, Nigeria

### 第2条 Goods

LED work lamps, model NB-330, 30 W, quantity 6,000 sets, packed in individual colour boxes
according to Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 88,200.
3.2 Trade term: **CFR Lagos, Incoterms® 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods from Ningbo Port not later than 10 December 2026.
4.2 The risk of loss of or damage to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Goods are on board
the vessel at the port of shipment.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer at its own expense.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Lagos.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 20% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance of 80% by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of Singapore.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Contract shall be finally settled by
arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) under the SIAC
Rules, and the award shall be final and binding on both parties.
9.3 Alternatively, the Buyer may refer the dispute to the courts of Singapore.
