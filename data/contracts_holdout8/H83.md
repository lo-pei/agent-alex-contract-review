**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H83
**Date**: 19 October 2026
**Place**: Zhuhai, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Zhuhai Golden Harvest Foods Co., Ltd., Zhuhai, China
**Buyer**: Harbour Foods Pty Ltd., Sydney, Australia

### 第2条 Goods

Canned straw mushrooms, 425 g per tin, 4,800 cartons, specification and lot numbers according
to Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 52,300.
3.2 Trade term: **CFR Sydney, Incoterms® 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods from Zhuhai Port not later than 25 November 2026.
4.2 The risk of loss of or damage to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Goods are on board
the vessel at the port of shipment.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer at its own expense.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods immediately upon discharge at the port of destination.
6.2 Any defect, shortage or damage shall be notified to the Seller on the same day.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration in Sydney
under the ACICA Arbitration Rules, and the award shall be final and binding.
