**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H77
**Date**: 6 October 2026
**Place**: Nanjing, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Nanjing Sunward Home Textiles Co., Ltd., Nanjing, China
**Buyer**: Gulf Living Trading LLC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates

### 第2条 Goods

Cotton cushion covers, model NJ-902, size 45 × 45 cm, quantity 12,000 pieces,
packed in export cartons according to Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 42,600.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Tianjin, Incoterms® 2020**.
3.3 The ocean freight from Tianjin to Jebel Ali is on the Seller's account.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer at Tianjin Port
not later than 20 November 2026.
4.2 The risk of loss of or damage to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Goods are on board
the vessel.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer at its own expense.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Jebel Ali.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be referred to arbitration in London under the
LCIA Rules, and the arbitral award shall be final and binding on both parties.
