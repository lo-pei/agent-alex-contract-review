**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H89
**Date**: 2 November 2026
**Place**: Xiamen, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Xiamen Harbour Ceramics Co., Ltd., Xiamen, China
**Buyer**: Gulf Interior Trading LLC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates

### 第2条 Goods

Glazed porcelain floor tiles, model CT-660, 600 x 600 mm, quantity 22,000 square metres,
packed in wooden crates on pallets.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 148,500.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Xiamen, Incoterms® 2020**.
3.3 The price is inclusive of all freight charges up to Jebel Ali.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer at Xiamen
Port not later than 20 December 2026.
4.2 The Buyer shall give the Seller at least 14 days' notice of the vessel name and laycan.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer at its own cost.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Jebel Ali.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment shall be made by 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing,
and the balance of 70% by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration administered
by SIAC under the SIAC Rules, and the award shall be final and binding.
