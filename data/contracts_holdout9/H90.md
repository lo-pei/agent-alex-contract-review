**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H90
**Date**: 3 November 2026
**Place**: Ningbo, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Ningbo Sunrise Stationery Manufacturing Co., Ltd., Ningbo, China
**Buyer**: Nordic Office Supplies AB, Gothenburg, Sweden

### 第2条 Goods

Recycled paper notebooks, A5 size, 96 sheets, quantity 60,000 pieces, packed in cartons of
50 pieces.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: EUR 54,000.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Ningbo, Incoterms® 2020**.
3.3 The Buyer shall arrange and pay for the ocean freight from Ningbo to Gothenburg.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods not later than 10 January 2027.
4.2 The risk of loss of or damage to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Goods are on board
the vessel at Ningbo Port.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Buyer shall insure the Goods from the time the risk passes.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 21 days after arrival at Gothenburg.
6.2 Any claim for defects shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment shall be made by 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing,
and the balance of 70% by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of Sweden.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration administered
by the Arbitration Institute of the Stockholm Chamber of Commerce, and the award shall be
final and binding.
9.3 Either party may instead elect to have any dispute decided by the courts of Singapore.
