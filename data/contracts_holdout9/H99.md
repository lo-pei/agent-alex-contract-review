**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H99
**Date**: 12 November 2026
**Place**: Rotterdam, the Netherlands

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Rotterdam Cocoa Processing B.V., Rotterdam, the Netherlands
**Buyer**: Guangzhou Chocolate Manufacturing Co., Ltd., Guangzhou, China

### 第2条 Goods

Alkalised cocoa powder, 10-12% fat content, quantity 25,000 kg, packed in 25 kg multi-wall
paper bags.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: EUR 87,500.
3.2 Trade term: **CFR Guangzhou, Incoterms® 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods from Rotterdam Port not later than 10 January 2027.
4.2 The risk of loss of or damage to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Goods are on board
the vessel at the port of shipment.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Buyer shall insure the Goods from the time the risk passes, for 110% of the invoice
value against Institute Cargo Clauses (A).

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Guangzhou.
6.2 Any claim for defects shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment shall be made by 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing,
and the balance of 70% by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the Netherlands.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration in
Rotterdam under the NAI Arbitration Rules, and the award shall be final and binding.
