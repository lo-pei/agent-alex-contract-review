**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H95
**Date**: 8 November 2026
**Place**: Busan, Korea

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Busan Marine Foods Co., Ltd., Busan, Korea
**Buyer**: Seattle Seafood Trading Inc., Seattle, United States

### 第2条 Goods

Frozen mackerel fillets, IQF, 1 kg vacuum bags, quantity 40,000 kg.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 132,000.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Busan, Incoterms® 2020**.
3.3 The Goods shall be packed in one 40-foot container (FCL) and delivered to the container
yard nominated by the Buyer.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods to the container yard not later than 15 December 2026.
4.2 The risk of loss of or damage to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Goods are on board
the vessel at Busan Port.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be arranged by the Buyer.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 7 days after arrival at Seattle.
6.2 Any claim for temperature damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment shall be made by 20% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing,
and the balance of 80% by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration administered
by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, and the award
shall be final and binding.
