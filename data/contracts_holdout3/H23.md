**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H23
**Date**: 14 October 2025
**Place**: Guangzhou, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Guangzhou Sanhe Industrial Co., Ltd., Guangzhou, China
**Buyer**: Antwerp Metals N.V., Antwerp, Belgium

### 第2条 Goods

Cold-rolled steel sheets, grade SPCC, quantity 500 metric tons, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 372,000.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Antwerp Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 Seller shall ship the goods at Guangzhou Port on or before 20 December 2025.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Seller shall effect insurance covering Institute Cargo Clauses (C) in favour of Buyer
and deliver the policy with the shipping documents.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 Buyer shall inspect the goods within 30 days after arrival at Antwerp.
6.2 Notice of non-conformity shall be given in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment: by irrevocable documentary credit available 60 days after the bill of lading date.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 The affected party shall notify the other party in writing within 15 days after the
force majeure event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration at the China International Economic
and Trade Arbitration Commission.
