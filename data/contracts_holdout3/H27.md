**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H27
**Date**: 21 November 2025
**Place**: Shanghai, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Shanghai Haoyu Machinery Co., Ltd., Shanghai, China
**Buyer**: Ligurian Machinery S.r.l., Genoa, Italy

### 第2条 Goods

Industrial compressors, model HY-75, quantity 18 units, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Unit price: USD 5,900 per unit.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Shanghai Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 Seller shall place the goods on board the vessel nominated by Buyer at Shanghai Port
on or before 10 January 2026.

### 第5条 Freight and Insurance

5.1 Sea freight and marine insurance shall be borne by the Buyer.
5.2 Seller shall insure the goods against all risks for **110% of the CIF value** for the
benefit of Buyer, and the policy shall be assignable.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 Buyer shall inspect the goods within 30 days after arrival at Genoa.
6.2 Notice of non-conformity shall be given in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment: 40% of the contract price by T/T in advance, and the balance against a copy
of the bill of lading.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Written notice of a force majeure event shall be given within 15 days.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration at the China International Economic
and Trade Arbitration Commission.
9.3 The award shall be final and binding, and judgment on the award may be enforced in any
court of competent jurisdiction.
