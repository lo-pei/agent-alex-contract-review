**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H19
**Date**: 26 September 2025
**Place**: Dalian, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Dalian Beichen Heavy Industry Co., Ltd., Dalian, China
**Buyer**: Aegean Shipping Supply S.A., Piraeus, Greece

### 第2条 Goods

Steel wire ropes, model BC-18, quantity 900 coils, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 174,000.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Dalian Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 Seller shall place the goods on board the vessel nominated by Buyer at Dalian Port
on or before 5 November 2025.

### 第5条 Freight and Charges

5.1 All ocean freight and booking charges shall be paid by the seller up to the port of
destination Piraeus.
5.2 Discharge costs and import clearance charges shall be borne by Buyer.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 Buyer shall inspect the goods within 14 days after arrival at Piraeus.
6.2 Notice of non-conformity shall be given in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment: 30% of the contract price by T/T as deposit, and the balance against the
shipping documents.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 The affected party shall give written notice within 15 days after the force majeure
event and provide evidence.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration at the China International Economic
and Trade Arbitration Commission in Beijing.
