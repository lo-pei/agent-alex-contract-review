5.1 Buyer shall inspect the goods within 21 days after receipt and give written notice of
any defect within that period.

6.1 Payment: 50% of the contract price by T/T before shipment, and the balance against
a copy of the bill of lading.

### 第7条 Force Majeure

7.1 A party affected by force majeure shall give written notice within 10 days and provide
evidence issued by a competent authority.

### 第8条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

8.1 This contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
8.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration in Singapore administered by the
Singapore International Arbitration Centre.
8.3 The arbitral award shall be final. If the losing party considers the award unjust, it
may file a lawsuit before the competent court within 60 days after the award is issued.
