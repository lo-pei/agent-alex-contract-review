### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Ningbo Tianhe Industrial Co., Ltd., Ningbo, China
**Buyer**: Rotterdam Offshore Services B.V., Rotterdam, the Netherlands

### 第2条 Goods

Marine winches, model TH-58, quantity 30 units, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 186,000.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Rotterdam Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 Seller shall ship the goods at Ningbo Port on or before 15 August 2025.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Seller shall insure the goods against all risks with an insured value of USD 204,600,
being 110% of the contract price, and the policy shall be assignable to Buyer.

### 第6条 Inspection

6.1 Buyer shall inspect the goods within 30 days after arrival at Rotterdam.
6.2 Notice of any non-conformity shall be given within a reasonable time after discovery.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment: 20% deposit by T/T within 7 days after signing, and the balance by
irrevocable L/C at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 The affected party shall notify the other party in writing within 15 days after the
force majeure event and provide supporting evidence.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration in Rotterdam under the rules of the
Netherlands Arbitration Institute.
9.3 Judgment on the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.
