### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Yantai Zhenhua Machinery Co., Ltd., Yantai, China
**Buyer**: Hanjin Industrial Supply Co., Ltd., Busan, Republic of Korea

### 第2条 Goods

Hydraulic presses, model YZ-320, quantity 15 units, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Unit price: USD 7,400 per unit.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Busan Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 Seller shall ship the goods at Yantai Port on or before 20 July 2025.
4.2 Risk passes to Buyer when the goods are on board the vessel.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Seller shall insure the goods against all risks with an **insured value of 110% of the invoice value**.
5.2 The insurance policy shall be assignable to Buyer.

### 第6条 Inspection

6.1 Buyer shall inspect the goods within 14 days after arrival at Busan.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment: 30% of the contract price by T/T before shipment, and the balance by
irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party is liable for delay caused by force majeure, provided that the affected
party gives written notice within 15 days.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 If friendly negotiation fails, the Buyer may choose to refer the dispute to arbitration
in Seoul, or to litigate before the courts of the Republic of Korea.
9.3 The arbitral award shall be final and binding on both parties.
