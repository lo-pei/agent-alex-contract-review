**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H119
**Date**: 22 November 2026
**Place**: Qingdao, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Qingdao Beichen Rubber Products Co., Ltd., Qingdao, China
**Buyer**: Andean Mining Supply SpA, Antofagasta, Chile

### 第2条 Goods

Steel-cord conveyor belts, model BC-1200, quantity 1,800 metres, packed in wooden reels.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 144,000.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Antofagasta, Incoterms® 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods from Qingdao Port not later than 23 December 2026.
4.2 The Buyer shall give the Seller at least 14 days' notice of the vessel name and laycan.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods under Institute Cargo Clauses (A), and the insurance
policy shall be transferred to the Buyer with the bill of lading.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after discharge at Antofagasta.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing;
the balance of 70% shall be paid against a copy of the bill of lading.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided
that written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of Singapore.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration
administered by SIAC under the SIAC Rules.
