**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H124
**Date**: 27 November 2026
**Place**: Ningbo, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Ningbo Haishu Kitchenware Co., Ltd., Ningbo, China
**Buyer**: Iberia Household Goods SL, Valencia, Spain

### 第2条 Goods

Stainless steel cookware sets, model HS-CS8, quantity 12,000 sets, packed in export cartons.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: EUR 96,000.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Valencia, Incoterms® 2020**.
3.3 The Seller shall arrange ocean freight to Valencia and shall pay all loading and export
clearance costs.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods from Ningbo Port not later than 26 December 2026.
4.2 The Buyer shall give the Seller at least 14 days' notice of the vessel name and laycan.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods for 110% of the invoice value against Institute Cargo
Clauses (A), and the insurance policy shall be transferred to the Buyer with the bill of lading.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after discharge at Valencia.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay by irrevocable L/C at sight opened within ten days after signing the
Contract.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided
that written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of Singapore.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration
administered by SIAC under the SIAC Rules.
9.3 The award shall be final and binding on both parties.
