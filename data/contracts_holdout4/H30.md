**Contract No.**: H30
**Date**: 12 January 2026
**Place**: Zhuhai, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Zhuhai Oceanic Equipment Co., Ltd., Zhuhai, China
**Buyer**: Malacca Marine Services Sdn. Bhd., Port Klang, Malaysia

### 第2条 Goods

Ship cranes, model ZO-90, quantity 5 units, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 233,000.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Zhuhai Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 Seller shall place the goods on board the vessel nominated by Buyer at Zhuhai Port
on or before 10 April 2026.

### 第5条 Costs

5.1 The ocean freight to Port Klang shall be paid by the Seller, and the booking charges
shall also be for the Seller's account.
5.2 Discharge costs at Port Klang shall be borne by Buyer.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 Buyer shall inspect the goods within 21 days after arrival at Port Klang.
6.2 Notice of non-conformity shall be given in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 Payment: 30% of the contract price by T/T in advance, and the balance against a copy
of the shipping documents.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 The affected party shall give written notice within 15 days after the force majeure
event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration at the China International Economic
and Trade Arbitration Commission.
