**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H65
**Date**: 14 July 2026
**Place**: Wenzhou, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Wenzhou Lanyard Textiles Co., Ltd., Wenzhou, China
**Buyer**: Kestrel Promotions Ltd., Manchester, United Kingdom

### 第2条 Goods

Polyester lanyards with metal clip, model WZ-118, quantity 80,000 pieces, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 36,400.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Ningbo, Incoterms 2020**.
3.3 The ocean freight from Ningbo to Felixstowe shall be for the Seller's account, and its cost
has been included in the price stated in Clause 3.1.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer at Ningbo Port
not later than 20 September 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be effected by the Buyer at its own expense.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Felixstowe.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be referred to arbitration in London under the
LCIA Rules, and the arbitral award shall be final and binding on both parties.
