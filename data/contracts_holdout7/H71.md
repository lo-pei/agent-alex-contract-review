**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H71
**Date**: 25 September 2026
**Place**: Xiamen, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Fujian Xinsheng Footwear Co., Ltd., Xiamen, China
**Buyer**: Shoeworks GmbH, Hamburg, Germany

### 第2条 Goods

Running shoes, model XS-620, quantity 18,000 pairs, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price, Trade Term and Packing

3.1 Total contract price: USD 216,000.
3.2 Trade term: **FOB Xiamen, Incoterms 2020**.
3.3 The Goods shall be stuffed into two 40-foot full container load (FCL) containers at the
Xiamen container yard, and the Seller shall be responsible for loading and sealing.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer at Xiamen
Port not later than 10 December 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 Insurance shall be effected by the Buyer at its own expense.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 21 days after arrival at Hamburg.
6.2 Any claim for shortage or damage shall be notified in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 The affected party shall give written notice within 15 days after the force majeure event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be submitted to the Xiamen Arbitration
Commission for arbitration in accordance with its rules. The award shall be final.
