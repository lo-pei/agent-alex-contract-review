**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H69
**Date**: 12 September 2026
**Place**: Changzhou, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Jiangsu Tianhe Medical Devices Co., Ltd., Changzhou, China
**Buyer**: MedSupply Norden AB, Stockholm, Sweden

### 第2条 Goods

Sterile surgical gloves, model TH-S3, quantity 240,000 pairs, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: USD 92,500.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Stockholm, Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods at Shanghai Port on or before 15 December 2026.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods for 110% of the contract price against ICC(C) risks and
hand the policy over to the Buyer with the shipping documents.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 30 days after arrival at Stockholm.
6.2 Notice of non-conformity shall be given in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 20% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 The affected party shall give written notice within 14 days after the force majeure event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be referred to arbitration administered by the
Singapore International Arbitration Centre under the SIAC Rules.
9.3 The award shall be final and binding on both parties. Nevertheless, a party that is
dissatisfied with the award may, within ninety (90) days after the award is issued, bring the
matter before the courts of Singapore.
