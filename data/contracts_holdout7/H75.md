### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Suzhou Evergreen Packaging Machinery Co., Ltd., Suzhou, China
**Buyer**: Hanseatic Bottling B.V., Rotterdam, Netherlands

### 第2条 Goods

Automatic bottle filling line, model EG-24, quantity 2 sets, specification per Annex A.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Total contract price: EUR 385,000.
3.2 Trade term: **CIF Rotterdam, Incoterms 2020**.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 The Seller shall ship the Goods at Shanghai Port on or before 20 January 2027.

### 第5条 Insurance

5.1 The Seller shall insure the Goods for 110% of the contract price against ICC(C) risks and
hand the policy over to the Buyer with the shipping documents.

### 第6条 Inspection and Notice

6.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 14 days after arrival at Rotterdam.
6.2 Notice of non-conformity shall be given in writing within that period.

### 第7条 Payment

7.1 The Buyer shall pay 30% of the contract price by T/T within ten days after signing, and the
balance by irrevocable letter of credit at sight.

### 第8条 Force Majeure

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that
written notice is given within 15 days after the event.

### 第9条 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the Netherlands.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration administered
by the Netherlands Arbitration Institute (NAI) in accordance with its rules.
9.3 The award shall be final and binding on both parties.
