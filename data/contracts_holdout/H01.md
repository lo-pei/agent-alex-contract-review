**SALES CONTRACT**

**Contract No.**: H01
**Date**: 12 March 2025
**Place**: Ningbo, China

### 第1条 Parties

**Seller**: Ningbo Hengyuan Pumps Co., Ltd., Ningbo, China
**Buyer**: Adriatic Marine Supply Ltd., Piraeus, Greece

### 第2条 Goods

Industrial centrifugal pumps, model HY-200, quantity 40 units, specification per Annex I.

### 第3条 Price and Trade Term

3.1 Unit price: USD 2,150 per unit **FOB Ningbo Incoterms 2020**.
3.2 Total contract price: USD 86,000.

### 第4条 Delivery

4.1 Seller shall deliver the goods on board the vessel nominated by Buyer at Ningbo Port on or before 30 April 2025.
4.2 Risk in the goods passes to Buyer when the goods are on board.

### 第5条 Inspection and Notice

5.1 Buyer shall inspect the goods within 14 days after arrival at Piraeus.
5.2 Buyer shall give written notice of any non-conformity within a reasonable time after discovery.

### 第6条 Payment

Payment shall be made by irrevocable letter of credit at sight, available against presentation of the shipping documents.

### 第7条 Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, provided that the affected party notifies the other party in writing within 15 days.

### 第8条 Governing Law

This contract shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

### 第9条 Dispute Resolution

9.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this contract shall be settled by friendly negotiation.
9.2 Failing such settlement, the claimant may **at its option either submit the dispute to the Shanghai International Arbitration Center for arbitration, or bring an action before the competent court** at the defendant's place of business.
9.3 An arbitral award shall be final and binding.
