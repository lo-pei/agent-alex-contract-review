INTERNATIONAL SALES CONTRACT

Contract No.: SC-2026-001
Date: 2026-10-20
Place: Shanghai, China

Seller: ABC Trading Co., Ltd., 88 Zhongshan Road, Shanghai, China
Buyer: Global Import LLC, 12 Hafenstrasse, Hamburg, Germany

1. Goods and Quantity
Seller agrees to sell and Buyer agrees to buy 5,000 metric tons of hot rolled steel coils, Grade Q235B, as described in Annex A.

2. Price and Payment
The unit price is USD 620 per metric ton FOB Shanghai. The total contract price is USD 3,100,000.
Payment shall be made by T/T as 100% advance payment within five (5) banking days after signing this Contract.

3. Delivery
Shipment shall be effected within 45 days after receipt of the advance payment. Partial shipment is not allowed.

4. Inspection
Inspection shall be carried out by SGS at the port of loading. The inspection certificate issued by SGS shall be final and binding upon both parties.

5. Warranty
Seller warrants that the goods are free from defects in material and workmanship for twelve (12) months from the date of shipment.

6. Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that the affected party gives written notice within fifteen (15) days.

7. Notices
All notices shall be in writing and sent by email to the addresses set out in Annex A.
